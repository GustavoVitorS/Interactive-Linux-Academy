# Community Code of Conduct

Interactive Linux Academy welcomes learners and contributors with different experience levels.

Please:

- discuss technical disagreements with evidence and respect;
- criticize code or explanations rather than people;
- assume questions may come from beginners;
- avoid harassment, discrimination, threats, spam and personal attacks;
- disclose conflicts or limitations when they materially affect technical guidance.

Maintainers may edit, close or remove contributions that make the project unsafe or hostile. Repeated abusive behavior may result in participation restrictions.
